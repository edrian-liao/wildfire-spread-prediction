🔍 The model identified three key variables that played a central role in the predicted fire expansion:
  • NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) – This measure of vegetation health and density is critical in determining available fuel. Areas with higher NDVI tend to support more intense and faster-spreading fires.
  • Wind Speed – Wind significantly impacts how quickly a fire can spread by carrying embers and intensifying flame propagation, especially in open terrain.
  • Elevation – Elevation influences both temperature and moisture levels in vegetation. Higher elevations often retain more moisture, while lower valleys can act as fire corridors under certain conditions.
